Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email the address in the footer.

Get it on Google Play

Free to start · No account · 17 interface languages · Android 7.0 and up

Is TokuGo free?

Yes. The free version gives you 9,400 words (the top 100 of every topic), all N5 and N4 kanji, hiragana, katakana, Kanji Basics, N5 and N4 grammar, the first 12 conversations, every practice exam set with a 7-day pause per level, the dictionary and three audio decks, with ads. Premium unlocks every word and kanji, N3–N1 grammar and all conversations, removes ads, and adds offline study and exam practice without the pause.

What does Premium unlock?

All 38,085 words, the N3 to N1 kanji, no ads, offline study, exam practice without the 7-day pause, and unlimited audio decks that can be downloaded as audio files. Premium is bought inside the app through Google Play; the plans and prices are listed there, and subscriptions can be cancelled any time from Google Play. Words you have already studied stay reviewable even if a subscription ends.

Does it work offline?

All content ships inside the app. On the free version, starting a study session, quiz or practice exam needs an internet connection, while Premium works fully offline. Speaking practice uses the phone's speech recogniser, which on most phones needs internet.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account, sign-up or password, and the developer has no server.

What if I change phones or uninstall the app?

Your study progress is stored on your phone only. Before switching phones or uninstalling, export a backup file from the side panel (Back up data), then import it on the new phone. Without it the progress is lost.

Which languages are supported?

The interface comes in 17 languages: Indonesian, English, Vietnamese, Nepali, Burmese, Filipino, Thai, Khmer, Mongolian, Sinhala, Bengali, Hindi, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese (Taiwan and Hong Kong), Korean and Japanese. Word and kanji meanings are complete in all of them except Japanese, which shows English meanings. Grammar lessons, conversations and exam explanations are available in 14 languages; in Simplified Chinese and Japanese they are shown in English. The translation language can be chosen separately from the interface language.

Where does the Japanese voice come from?

From your phone's text-to-speech engine, usually Google Text-to-speech, so no audio files are downloaded. If the phone has no Japanese voice yet, the app shows how to install one. Quality and speed differ between phones, which is why listening scores cannot be compared across devices.

Does the app record my voice?

No. Speaking practice sends your speech to your phone's speech-recognition service (usually Google's) and only receives the recognised text back. The app does not record, store or send audio to the developer. Microphone permission is only requested when you open speaking practice.

Are practice scores the same as official scores?

No. TokuGo scores are an estimate based on the percentage of correct answers, for tracking progress. The official exams use a different scaled scoring. The app shows the official bands (A1, A2.1, A2.2 for JFT-Basic; section minimums for the JLPT) so you know where you stand, without a pass or fail verdict.

What does Mastered mean?

The highest stage of a word or kanji. Pressing Know it on a card only takes it to Known; Mastered is reached by answering it correctly in quizzes on three different days. Mastered words keep being reviewed when due and drop back on a mistake.

Why is there no separate JFT-Basic word level?

The Japan Foundation does not publish a JFT-Basic vocabulary list. Word levels in TokuGo (N5 to N1) are an estimate from the old JLPT lists and usage frequency; JFT-Basic tests the same basic vocabulary as N5 and N4 plus workplace words, which live in the Workplace group.

Where do the words, kanji and questions come from?

Vocabulary and kanji from JMdict and KANJIDIC by the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (CC BY-SA 4.0), stroke order from KanjiVG (CC BY-SA 3.0), word frequency from wordfreq. Grammar lessons and all practice questions were written for TokuGo and audited before release; no official questions are copied.

Where do ads appear?

On list screens, occasionally when the app opens, and after a session ends. Never on card, quiz, kana or grammar lesson, speaking practice, kanji writing, audio player or exam question screens. Premium removes all of them.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 and up, phones and tablets. All content ships inside the app.

How do I contact the developer?

Email the address in the footer of this page. Reports of wrong words, meanings or questions are very welcome.
