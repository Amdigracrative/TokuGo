覚

Flashcards that know when you are about to forget

Every flashcard is scheduled with FSRS, a modern spaced repetition algorithm. You rate yourself and the app works out when the word comes back: in minutes, days or weeks. The next interval is printed on every button.

Don’t know5 minutes Unsure6 days Know it15 days
証

Mastered means proven

Every word climbs four stages. “Know it” on a card makes a word Known; to reach Mastered, you must answer it correctly in a quiz on 3 different days. Mastered words keep being tested when they fall due, and drop back if you get them wrong.

  1. New
  2. Learning
  3. Known
  4. Mastered

Free to start

The free version gives you 9,626 words straight away: the top 100 of every topic, covering 99% of JFT-Basic and 96% of JLPT N5 vocabulary, plus all 284 N5 and N4 kanji, with ads. Every mock exam and the whole dictionary are open to everyone.

Premium unlocks every word and the N3–N1 kanji, removes ads, and lets you study offline. On the free version, starting a study session or an exam needs an internet connection.

Your study records stay on your phone

No account, no sign-up, no password. The developer has no server: your study records stay on your phone and are never sent to the developer.

Ads, usage measurement and crash reports use Google services. The details are in the privacy policy.
