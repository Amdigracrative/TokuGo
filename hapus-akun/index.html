Hapus akun TokuGo

Aplikasi Android TokuGo · Amdigra Creative Dev · 17 November 2026

Ringkasnya. TokuGo bisa dipakai tanpa akun. Akun hanya dibuat kalau kamu ikut papan peringkat dengan akun Google. Halaman ini menjelaskan cara menghapus akun itu beserta datanya, lewat aplikasi atau lewat email.

1. Hapus lewat aplikasi (langsung)

  1. Buka TokuGo, lalu buka menu samping (tombol ☰ di kiri atas).
  2. Ketuk baris akunmu di bagian atas menu (avatar dan nama panggilanmu).
  3. Ketuk Hapus akun, lalu Hapus.
  4. Pilih akun Google yang sama sekali lagi untuk memastikan.

Akun dan datanya langsung dihapus.

2. Hapus tanpa aplikasi (lewat email)

Kalau aplikasinya sudah tidak terpasang atau kamu tidak bisa membukanya:

  1. Kirim email ke amdigracrative@gmail.com dari alamat Google yang kamu pakai untuk papan peringkat.
  2. Tulis subjek Hapus akun TokuGo.
  3. Sebutkan nama panggilanmu di papan peringkat.

Kami menghapus akunmu paling lambat 30 hari sesudah email diterima dan membalas emailmu setelah selesai.

3. Data yang dihapus

  • Akun Firebase Authentication: alamat email dan ID akun Google-mu yang tersimpan di sana.
  • Data papan peringkat: nama panggilan, nomor avatar, XP minggu ini, XP total, dan waktu kiriman terakhir.
  • Laporan nama yang pernah kamu kirim tentang pengguna lain.

Semuanya dihapus seluruhnya; tidak ada salinan yang kami simpan sesudahnya.

4. Data yang tidak ikut terhapus

  • Data di HP-mu, termasuk progres belajar dan XP. Data itu tidak pernah dikirim ke kami. Hapus lewat menu samping → Cadangkan data → Mulai dari awal, atau dengan meng-uninstall aplikasi.
  • Cadangan di Google Drive-mu. Berkasnya ada di folder TokuGo di Drive-mu sendiri; hapus dari Google Drive kalau tidak diperlukan.
  • Data teknis di layanan Google (iklan, analitik, laporan kerusakan). Caranya ada di Kebijakan Privasi bagian 11.

Laporan dari pengguna lain tentang nama panggilanmu disimpan sampai selesai diperiksa, lalu dihapus.

Delete your TokuGo account

TokuGo Android app · Amdigra Creative Dev · 17 November 2026

In short. TokuGo works without an account. An account is created only if you join the leaderboard with a Google account. This page explains how to delete that account and its data, in the app or by email.

1. Delete in the app (immediately)

  1. Open TokuGo and open the side menu (the ☰ button at the top left).
  2. Tap your account row at the top of the menu (your avatar and nickname).
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete.
  4. Choose the same Google account once more to confirm.

The account and its data are deleted immediately.

2. Delete without the app (by email)

If the app is no longer installed or you cannot open it:

  1. Email amdigracrative@gmail.com from the Google address you used for the leaderboard.
  2. Use the subject Delete TokuGo account.
  3. State your leaderboard nickname.

We delete your account within 30 days of receiving the email and reply once it is done.

3. Data that is deleted

  • The Firebase Authentication account: the email address and Google account ID stored there.
  • Leaderboard data: nickname, avatar number, XP this week, total XP, and the time of the last update.
  • Any name reports you sent about other users.

All of it is deleted completely; we keep no copy afterwards.

4. Data that is not deleted

  • Data on your phone, including study progress and XP. It was never sent to us. Delete it through the side menu → Back up data → Start over, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Backups in your Google Drive. The files are in the TokuGo folder in your own Drive; delete them from Google Drive if you no longer need them.
  • Technical data in Google services (ads, analytics, crash reports). See Privacy Policy section 11.

Reports from other users about your nickname are kept until they have been reviewed, then deleted.
